At Standby Labs, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains, in simple terms, what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it when you visit or use www.standbylabs.in or contact us.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, such as:

  • Your Name
  • Phone Number
  • Email address
  • Company/business Name
  • Project or service requirements
  • Any other information you choose to provide through our forms, email, WhatsApp, or other communication channels

We may also automatically collect basic technical information when you visit our website, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and general website usage information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and requests

  • Provide and manage our services
  • Communicate with you about projects, services, or support
  • Improve our website and services
  • Maintain website security and prevent misuse
  • Meet legal or regulatory requirements when applicable

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality, understand website usage, and provide a better experience.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

4. Sharing Your Information

We may share information only when reasonably necessary to operate our business or provide our services.
This may include sharing information with:

  • Hosting and technology providers

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Our website or services may contain links to or integrate with third-party websites, tools, APIs, payment gateways, or other services.

These third parties have their own privacy policies, and Standby Labs is not responsible for how they collect or use information.

We recommend reviewing their privacy policies before providing them with personal information.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure.

However, no method of storing or transmitting information over the internet is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or protect our legitimate interests.

When information is no longer required, we may securely delete or anonymize it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information where legally permitted
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not intentionally directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent.
